# DPDP compliance kit: drafts for Updaate Computers

**Status: DRAFT, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.** Prepared by the IMS QA/security developer from public sources. Every
item here needs review by Updaate Computers and a qualified lawyer before it is used with real people. Fill the `<...>`
placeholders. Passing technical tests does not make the business legally compliant.

Key dates (DPDP Act 2023 commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E), 13 Nov 2025, and DPDP Rules 2025):
- **13 Nov 2025:** Board, definitions and Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 in force.
- **13 Nov 2026:** consent-manager rule in force.
- **13 May 2027:** notice, consent, children, security, breach, erasure and rights duties (ss.3–17, Rules 3, 5–16, 22, 23).

MeitY consulted on bringing 13 May 2027 forward to 13 Nov 2026. No amendment had been reported as notified by 23 Nov
2026, so re-check the Gazette before relying on this date. Until 13 May 2027, IT Act s.43A, the SPDI Rules 2011 and
the CERT-In Directions of 28 Apr 2022 apply.

## 1. Privacy notice (English draft)

Use it at the enquiry desk (printed or as a QR code), on the admission form and on the website. It must be
separate from other terms and written in plain language. Offer it in English and Marathi (DPDP s.5(3) and the
Eighth Schedule languages).

> **Updaate Computers — how we use your personal data**
>
> **Who we are.** Updaate Computers, `<address>`, `<phone>`, `<email>`. We decide how your data is used (we are
> the "Data Fiduciary").
>
> **What we collect and why.**
> - *Course enquiry:* your name, mobile number, optional email and address, the course you are interested in, and how
>   you heard of us. We use these to answer your enquiry and follow it up.
> - *Admission:* your name, your parent's or guardian's name, date of birth, gender, mobile and WhatsApp
>   number, address, education, chosen course, batch and fees. We use these to run your course, collect fees, issue
>   receipts and certificates, and contact you about classes and dues.
> - *Computer and online-form services:* your name, mobile number, the service you ask for, documents you hand us
>   for that job, and, if you choose, the username of an online account we create for you. We use these only to
>   complete that job and bill it. We do **not** keep online-account passwords. A password is printed on your bill
>   only if you ask for it.
> - We do **not** collect Aadhaar numbers or Aadhaar card copies.
>
> **Who else handles it.** Our software runs on a cloud database (Supabase, hosted in Sydney, Australia). If you
> agree, receipts and bills are sent to your WhatsApp through Meta's WhatsApp Business service. We do not sell your
> data or use it for advertising.
>
> **How long we keep it.** Enquiries: `<12 months>` after the last contact. Student records: `<n years>` after the
> course ends. Fee receipts and bills: the period that tax law requires (GST: 72 months from the due date of the
> annual return). Documents for a service job: `<30 days>` after the job is completed. After that, we erase the
> data, and we tell you at least 48 hours before (from 13 May 2027).
>
> **Your rights.** You can ask us what data we hold about you, have it corrected or updated, have it erased when
> we no longer need to keep it, withdraw your consent (as easily as you gave it), and name someone to act for you.
> Contact `<name / designation>`, `<phone>`, `<email>`, or ask at the desk. We will reply within `<15>` days and in
> any case within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India
> (contact details to be confirmed from the Board's official notice).
>
> **Children.** If the student is under 18, a parent or lawful guardian must agree on the student's behalf.

>
> **18 वर्षांखालील विद्यार्थी.** पालक किंवा कायदेशीर पालकाची संमती आवश्यक आहे.

## 3. Consent and guardian wording (admission form, draft)

Use separate tick boxes. None of them is pre-ticked, and the course does not depend on the optional ones.

- [ ] I have read the privacy notice. I agree that Updaate Computers may use the details on this form to run my
      course, collect fees and contact me about it. *(needed for admission)*
- [ ] Send my fee receipts and reminders on WhatsApp to `<number>`. *(optional)*
- [ ] **Student under 18:** I, `<guardian name>`, am the parent or lawful guardian of `<student>`, and I give
      this consent on the student's behalf. Guardian's mobile: `<...>`. ID seen: `<type only — not the number;
      not Aadhaar>`. Signature / date. *(needed if under 18)*

Record of consent kept by the institute: the date, the staff member who received it, and the notice version.
In the app this needs a small schema change (see the tracker, P1-03). Until then, keep the signed paper form.

Legal question for the lawyer: does Updaate Computers count as an "educational institution" under the Fourth
Schedule (Part A) of the DPDP Rules? That exemption only covers tracking and behavioural monitoring for educational
activities or safety. It does not cover consent for admission and fees, so plan for verifiable guardian consent
(Rule 10) either way.

## 5. Personal-data breach response (runbook)

**Never send a real notification during a test or drill.** Use the fixed text "DRILL — NOT A REAL INCIDENT" and
internal addresses only.

1. **Detect and record (0 h).** Open an entry in the incident register: the time noticed, who noticed it, and what was seen.
2. **Contain (0–2 h).** Disable the affected accounts. Rotate the leaked secrets: database password, `DATA_ENC_KEY` (only with a
   re-encryption plan), WhatsApp token. Take the server off-line if data is still leaking. Preserve logs; do not delete anything.
3. **Assess (within 6 h).** Work out which data, how many people, whether minors are affected, whether it was encrypted, and
   whether it is still exposed.
4. **CERT-In (within 6 h of noticing).** Under the CERT-In Directions of 28 Apr 2022, a body corporate (which includes a firm or sole
   proprietorship) reports specified incidents, including data breaches and leaks, at incident@cert-in.org.in using the
   CERT-In form. The decision to report belongs to the owner.
5. **DPDP (from 13 May 2027; Rule 7).** Tell each affected person without delay, in plain language: what happened, the likely
   impact, what has been done, what they should do, and who to contact. Tell the Data Protection Board without delay, then send
   a detailed report within **72 hours** of becoming aware.
6. **Recover and review.** Fix the cause, restore from backup if needed, record the lessons learned, and close the register entry.

## 6. Data-principal requests (access, correction, erasure, nomination, grievance)

1. Receive the request at the desk, by phone or by email. Log it in the privacy-request register the same day.
2. Verify identity without collecting Aadhaar. Match the mobile number (one-time code or call-back) and the name on record. For
   a minor, check that the person asking is the guardian on record.
3. Access: give a summary of the data held (the Student Profile screen, printed). Correction: edit it in IMS (this is audited).
4. Erasure: if there is no legal duty to keep the data (fee and tax records are kept), anonymise the profile (P1-04) and
   tell the person.
5. Reply within the published period (suggested 15 days, never more than 90). Record the outcome and close the entry.
